Each funnel follows one source from first contact to a sale. The percentage shows how many made it from the step above. Best converting source first.

How the steps are counted: Opted in = has a deal in the pipeline. Qualified = opted in and not disqualified. Call booked = reached the booking stage or beyond. Call happened = closed, in follow up, or lost after the call (cancelled, no-show and calls still to come are not counted). Sale closed = in the Closed stage.
